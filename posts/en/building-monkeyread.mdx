[monkeyread](https://github.com/damien-mathieu1/monkeyread)
([monkeyread.eu](https://monkeyread.eu)) is a free RSVP speed reader with
the look and feel of [monkeytype](https://monkeytype.com). Words appear one
at a time in the same spot, with a highlighted pivot letter your eye lands
on, and the whole UX — layout, config bar, focus mode, themes — borrows
directly from monkeytype's.

## What it does

- RSVP reading: one word (or up to three) at a time, pivot letter aligned on
  a fixed guide, longer pauses on punctuation
- Any speed, with presets or a custom value
- Focus mode: everything but the current word fades out
- `space` play/pause, `tab` restart, `←`/`→` to step a word at a time
- A library: import PDF, EPUB, or TXT, parsed client-side with pdf.js and
  JSZip, kept in IndexedDB along with your reading position
- 5 themes (serika dark, serika, paper, nord, dracula), 10 fonts, French and
  English

Texts and books never leave your browser. An optional account only syncs
reading stats and settings, not the content you're reading.

## Stack

Next.js, Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui. Linting and formatting run on oxlint
and oxfmt, with a `shadcn/no-arbitrary-values` rule that keeps app code on
the design tokens in `globals.css` instead of one-off Tailwind values.

## Why

I wanted to read faster without installing anything, and monkeytype already
nailed the kind of minimal, keyboard-first interface that disappears once
you're using it. Pointing that same interaction model at RSVP reading felt
obvious once I'd used monkeytype enough to have it memorized.
